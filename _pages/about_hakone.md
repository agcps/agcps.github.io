• 2nd prize among PhD oral presentations chosen by Organizing Committees at HAKONE XIX International Symposium on High Pressure Low Temperature Plasma Chemistry

• Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS) can be effectively treated by atmospheric pressure plasmas (APPs) through electrons, ions, excited species, photons, and solvated electrons. The simulation results are compared to experimental results in terms of PFOA degradation and defluorination rates.

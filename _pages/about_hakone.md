• 2nd prize among PhD oral presentations chosen by Organizing Committees at HAKONE XIX International Symposium on High Pressure Low Temperature Plasma Chemistry

• Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS) are highly persistent in the environment and can be effectively treated by atmospheric pressure plasmas (APPs) through electrons, ions, radicals photons, and solvated electrons. The simulation showed a good agreement with experiments in terms of PFOA degradation and defluorination rates.

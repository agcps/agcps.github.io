• 2nd prize, Best PhD Oral Presentation by Organizing Committees' Choice at HAKONE XIX International Symposium on High Pressure Low Temperature Plasma Chemistry

• Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS) are highly persistent in the environment and atmospheric pressure plasmas (APPs) have shown effective treatment of certain PFAS at the gas-liquid interface by the incident flux of electrons, ions, radicals and photons at the plasma-liquid interface and solvated electrons. The simulation regarding PFOA degradation and defluorination rates matched with experiments.
